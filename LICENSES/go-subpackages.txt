Licences of third-party code in subdirectories of linked Go modules
====================================================================

Some Go modules the backend links carry code under a licence of their own in
a subdirectory, separate from the module's main licence. Those licences are
reproduced verbatim below, one section per directory, as their terms require
for binary distribution. NOTICE lists the same directories.
scripts/licenses/check.sh fails when a linked directory licence is missing
here or from NOTICE.
