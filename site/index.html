Run your restaurant on software you own.

Payverge is an open-source, self-hostable restaurant operating system with AI built in. QR ordering, a kitchen display, bills and payments, reservations and delivery, on your own server, with no platform fees.

What it does

One system from the table to the books

Guest ordering, the kitchen, payments and the back office share one database, so an order placed at table 4 is on the kitchen screen, the bill and the reports at the same moment.

  • QR menus and table ordering

    Each table gets its own QR code. Guests open the menu in the browser, order, call a server and pay from their phone. There is nothing to install.

  • AI waiter in the guest's language

    Answers menu questions in any of the 21 guest languages, works from the allergens listed on your menu, leaves drinks with alcohol out when a guest asks for alcohol-free, and only adds dishes that are on your menu and in stock.

  • Kitchen display

    Orders reach the kitchen screen live and move from new to cooking to ready. Print kitchen tickets and bills on 58 or 80 mm thermal printers from the browser.

  • Bills and split payments

    Open a bill per table, split it evenly, by amount or by item, take tips, and close the day with a cash-drawer Z report.

  • Payments on your own accounts

    Connect your own Stripe, Mercado Pago or PayPal account, accept USDC on Base verified on-chain, or take cash at the counter. Payverge adds no fee of its own.

  • Reservations and delivery

    Online bookings that confirm automatically or wait for your approval, plus delivery orders with a tracking page that refreshes as the order moves.

  • Ops assistant and Director

    The Ops assistant answers how-to questions and checks your setup. The Director reads sales, menu engineering, labor and food cost, then proposes price or availability changes for you to approve.

  • Back office

    Inventory, staff roles, schedules, time clock and tips, CRM and loyalty, accounting and analytics, plus ARCA e-invoicing for venues in Argentina (beta).

Try it locally in 5 minutes

On any machine with Docker and Git, the demo flag loads two fictional restaurants with menus, tables, bookings and orders, so every screen above has something on it.

Sign in with the admin email you pass and the password the script prints.

bash
$ git clone https://github.com/stdevMac/payverge.git
$ cd payverge
$ ./deploy/install.sh --domain localhost --admin-email you@example.com --demo
Then open https://localhost. Your browser will ask you to trust the local certificate; the local trial guide explains how.

AI built in

AI that works inside the lines you draw

The AI features are optional. When they are on, they answer from your data, ask before they change anything, and stay inside daily spend caps that are on by default.

  • Grounded in your menu

    The waiter quotes only dishes, prices and allergens from your live menu. The server checks every cart action, so an invented dish never reaches an order.

  • You approve every change

    Director suggestions for prices or availability wait for an owner to apply them. Each applied change can be undone for 24 hours, as long as the menu has not changed since.

  • Spend you control

    A ledger records what every AI call costs. Daily dollar caps per venue and for the whole server are on by default, and you set the amounts. The guest waiter also has a daily message limit. An evaluation harness regression-tests the prompts.

  • Off until you turn it on

    Until you connect a model, hosted or on your own server, there is no AI and no AI cost. Ordering, the kitchen, bills and payments work the same.

How it works

From an empty server to the first order

  1. 1

    Deploy

    Run the installer on a Linux server with Docker. Payverge comes up with PostgreSQL, automatic HTTPS and your admin account.

  2. 2

    Configure with the AI

    Photograph your paper menu and the importer turns it into a structured menu. The Ops assistant checks what is still missing and takes you to the right screen. Coding agents get an AGENTS.md guide to the codebase.

  3. 3

    Open your restaurant

    Print the table QR codes, put the kitchen display on a tablet, and take your first order.

Self-host

Deploy in one command

You need a Linux server with Docker and a domain pointed at it. The installer asks for the domain and an admin email, generates the secrets and starts the stack.

  • PostgreSQL, API, web app and Caddy from public container images, with HTTPS certificates issued automatically.
  • No outside accounts to start. Files stay on a local volume and email goes to the logs until you connect S3 or a mail provider.
  • Add integrations when you want them: payment providers, a model for the AI features (an OpenRouter key, or your own Ollama or vLLM server), Telegram alerts.
bash 
curl -fsSL https://github.com/stdevMac/payverge/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash
Prefer to read it first? Download install.sh from the latest release, read it, then run bash install.sh.
